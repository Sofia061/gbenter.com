Artist Rules

1. Mutual Respect & Communication

We value a friendly atmosphere, mutual respect, and open communication between artists and the team.

2. Professional Approach

Artists must be disciplined, responsible, and committed to improvement.

3. Coordination of Decisions

All important career decisions must be discussed with directors.

4. Confidentiality

All company information is strictly confidential and cannot be shared.

5. Experience

We expect training, performance experience, social media presence, achievements, and professionalism.

6. Artist Rating System

Artists are evaluated every 6 months. Low performance may lead to removal.

7. Clean-ups

Inactive or low-quality artists may be removed after review.

Prohibited Actions

Disrespect, rule violations, use of prototypes, joining other companies.

Incorrect applications will not be reviewed.

Only successful candidates will be contacted.

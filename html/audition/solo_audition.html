Audition Rules

Please read the rules carefully before you apply.

  1. Mutual Respect and Communication
    We value a friendly atmosphere, mutual respect, and open communication between artists and the team. Everyone should feel comfortable expressing their ideas and opinions.
  2. Professional Approach
    We expect our artists to demonstrate discipline, responsibility, and a commitment to continuous improvement. Punctuality and a serious attitude towards their work are essential for success.
  3. Coordination of Decisions
    All important matters concerning your career must be discussed with the directors and the team to prevent misunderstandings and ensure the effective development of your career.
  4. Confidentiality
    All information regarding the company and its activities is confidential. Sharing such information outside the team is strictly prohibited. The company will also never share your personal data with third parties without your explicit consent.
  5. Experience
    Experience is a key requirement for joining our team. We are looking for artists who demonstrate the following:

    1. Formal Training: Completed courses, workshops, or professional training in your respective field (e.g., music school for vocalists, dance school for dancers). Recognized certificates or diplomas are considered an advantage.

    2. Practical Experience: Participation in live performances, concerts, competitions, or festivals. Experience working with studios, agencies, or production companies. Previous recordings or releases of musical, dance, or other creative projects.

    3. Social Media Presence: Active promotion of your work on social media platforms (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, etc.). Collaborations with other creators or brands.

    4. Recognition and Awards: Awards, certificates, or honorary titles in your field. Positive reviews, feedback, or high audience engagement with your social media content.

    5. Professionalism and Responsibility: Ability to work in a team, follow instructions, and meet deadlines. Willingness to learn, adapt, and grow as part of our team.
  6. Artist Rating System
    Every six months, all artists are evaluated on a 10-point scale across the following categories:

    1. Activity and participation Quality of work
    2. Completion of assignments from the administration
    3. Meeting deadlines

    → If an artist receives an overall score below 5, they will receive a warning.
    → If the artist receives a score below 5 again in the next evaluation, they will be removed from the company.
  7. Team Reviews
    Team reviews are conducted according to the following rules:

    1. Frequency: Reviews are held regularly, but no fixed dates are established. The decision to conduct a review is made at the discretion of the directors.

    2. Criteria: Low activity or lack of participation in projects. Violation of company rules. Poor work quality or lack of professionalism. Lack of noticeable progress or skill development.

    3. Process: Artists being considered for removal will be notified. They will be given an opportunity to discuss their situation with a director. If the decision is confirmed, the artist will be removed from the team.
  8. Prohibited Actions
    1. Disrespectful behaviour towards the team.
    2. Insulting or offending other team members.
    3. Violating company rules.
    4. Being a member of other companies or groups affiliated with other companies.
  9. Notes
    Violation of these rules may result in removal from the company.
    Applications that are not completed correctly will not be considered.
    Only candidates who successfully pass the audition will be contacted.
    The contact time variates between a day to a week.
    Prototypes are not allowed.
    It is not allowed to be part of another company as an artist.

Audition Rules

Please read the rules carefully before you apply.

  1. A Positive Atmosphere
    We believe great teamwork grows from trust and mutual respect. We value kindness, professionalism, and a supportive environment for everyone.
  2. Mutual Respect
    We ask all team members, regardless of their position, to treat one another with fairness and respect.
  3. Respectful Communication
    We ask that everyone refrain from insulting colleagues or artists. Conflicts and disrespectful behavior cannot be accepted within our team.
  4. A Responsible Approach to Work
    We appreciate it when every staff member takes their duties seriously, meets deadlines, and delivers high-quality results.
  5. Required Skills

    1. Lyricists, beatmakers, designers, mixing engineers, and musical producers are expected to have experience working with professional software.

    2. PR-Producers are expected to actively support our artists and help with their promotion.

    3. Cooperation managers are expected to have experience in establishing and maintaining collaborations.

  6. Additional Guidelines for PR-Producers
    We ask you to submit a weekly report on your work progress. For example: purchased artwork for 3–4 posts for a specific artist, wrote a new large-scale article, prepared new practice materials, purchased ads, and much more. In case of posts promotion, you should show how it was before, and how it became after the promotion. You will get a reminder to submit your report weekly.

    If you are unable to submit a report, please let us know in advance. A missed report without prior notice will result in a warning.

    After 3 warnings, we will unfortunately have to part ways with the team member.

  7. Payment
    As a thank-you for their ongoing contribution, permanent staff members (PR-producers, video editors, cooperation managers) receive a weekly gift of 15 stars, depending on their activity during the week.

    Other staff members (lyricists, beatmakers, mixing engineers, musical producers, designers) receive a gift of 25–50 stars after each project, depending on the amount and complexity of the work.

    The payment may be raised depending on the amount of work done for the project or throughout the week.
  8. Prohibited Actions
    1. Starting disputes.
    2. Using offensive language.
    3. Not effectuating / ignoring assignments from the directors.
    4. Spreading rumors about the company or its artists.
  9. Notes
    We kindly ask everyone to follow these guidelines, as violations will result in removal from the company.
    Applications that are not completed correctly will not be considered.
    Only candidates who successfully pass the audition will be contacted.
    The contact time variates between a day to a week.

About GB ENTERTAINMENT

GB ENTERTAINMENT is an international online entertainment company and talent agency, creating and developing artists across multiple creative fields.

Founded in October 2022 by Golban Sofia, the company started as a small creative project and evolved into a multi-format platform supporting vocalists, dancers, models, and digital creators worldwide.

We produce original music, covers, dance performances, and entertainment content, while helping artists grow, gain exposure, and build long-term careers in the industry.

GB ENTERTAINMENT is more than a label — it is a launchpad for talent, combining creative development with global online promotion.

Talent has no borders.
